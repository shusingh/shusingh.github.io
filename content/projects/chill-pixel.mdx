## System intent

Chill Pixel is a lofi music player with a pixel-art theme: relaxing beats inside a nostalgic scene, built to be left open in a corner tab while you work. It is intentionally a mood piece rather than a feature-rich audio product; the design goal is that the whole page feels like one cohesive place, and the player controls dissolve into it.

## Requirements

- Streams a curated rotation of lofi tracks with simple, reliable playback controls.
- The pixel-art scene carries the atmosphere; UI chrome stays minimal and in-theme.
- Loads fast and runs quietly for hours as a background tab.
- Works at desktop and mobile widths without breaking the scene's composition.

## High-level design

A small React and TypeScript app built with Vite and styled with Tailwind CSS. Playback state lives in a single player store; the scene and controls render from it. There is no backend of its own: the app is a static bundle deployed on Render, which keeps hosting as low-maintenance as the product's ambitions.

## What I would improve

A richer scene that reacts to the time of day, user-selectable stations, and a tiny visualizer that stays within the pixel-art language rather than fighting it.
